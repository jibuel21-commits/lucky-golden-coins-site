Lucky Golden Coins

Support

Questions, problems or feedback? Email cardintel@outlook.com and include your device (for example, iPhone 16 or Pixel 9) and the app version, found in your device's app settings.

Common questions

How do I change the language? Tap the globe menu at the top of the screen. The app starts in your device's language when it's available.

How do I get a new reading? After a reading, tap Start over and flip again.

Does the app collect my data? No. Everything happens on your device. See the privacy policy.

Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (iPhone) or through Google Play (Android).
