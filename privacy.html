LionMorrow app privacy

Effective November 8, 2026 · Release candidate 1.0.1

This notice describes the LionMorrow downloadable app. The public website has a separate website privacy notice.

Coins and readings

Coin flips and readings are processed on your device. The app does not ask you to enter a question or personal details. It does not transmit coin results or maintain a saved reading history.

Language preference

The app saves your selected interface language in local storage on your device so it can remember your choice. That preference is not sent to us. Clearing the app's local data resets it.

Accounts, advertising, and analytics

The app has no account system, advertising, analytics service, or tracking SDK added by us. Its reading content, artwork, and fonts are bundled for offline use.

Support

If you choose to email support, your email address, message, and attachments are received through our email provider and used to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, full payment details, or sensitive personal information. Opening an external email or website uses that service under its own policies.

Store purchases and platform services

The store where you buy or download the app processes payment, download, and platform information under its own policies. We do not collect payment-card details inside the app. Platform diagnostics and purchase information made available by a store are governed by that platform's settings and policies.

Children

The app does not request personal information from children or other users. A parent or guardian can contact us about information a child may have sent through support email.

Changes and contact

This notice will be updated when the app's practices change. Privacy questions and requests concerning support messages can be sent to cardintel@outlook.com.
