Lucky Golden Coins Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Lucky Golden Coins is a coin-flipping fortune app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store, or send any personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

How the app works

Coin flips and readings happen entirely on your device. The app does not connect to the internet to work, and your coin results are not saved or shared.

Purchases

If you buy the app through the Apple App Store, Google Play, Microsoft Store or another store, that store handles your payment under its own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
